How earning works · the short version

Only deposited Squeeks count toward power. A Squeek sitting in your wallet earns nothing — deposit it in Staking from the official Sepolia app and its power starts earning immediately. Your power is your deposited Squeeks' base power plus your staked SQK's power, boosted by your Squeek power. Settled blocks only turn into issued SQK when someone calls checkpoint() on the SqueekMining contract — anyone can call it, it costs only Sepolia gas.

⚠️ Testnet SQK and test Squeeks have no monetary value. The real $SQK token has not launched — anything trading as SQK today is fake. Never share a seed phrase or private key with any site, including this one: SqueekWatch never asks for them.
